Practical 6: D flip-flop simulator

A model of one 74HC74 flip-flop. Q copies D only at the moment CLK goes from 0 to 1 (the rising edge). Try changing D while CLK is high: nothing happens to Q.

Inputs

Keyboard: press d to toggle D and c to toggle CLK.

Outputs

Q = 0
/Q = 1
