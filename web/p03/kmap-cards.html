Practical 3: K-map groups as cards

F(D,C,B,A) = 1 for the prime BCD digits 2, 3, 5 and 7. Codes 10 to 15 never occur, so they are "don't care" (X) and we may use them to make bigger groups.

The Karnaugh map

K-map of F. Rows are DC, columns are BA, both in Gray-code order.
DC \ BA00011110
000011
010110
11XXXX
1000XX

Blue cells form group 1, orange cells form group 2 (the colour is a hint only; the cards below say the same in words).

The groups

  • Group 1: B·C'

    Cells 2, 3, 10, 11 (a quad that uses two don't-cares).

    B is always 1 and C is always 0 inside the group.

  • Group 2: C·A

    Cells 5, 7, 13, 15 (a quad that uses two don't-cares).

    C is always 1 and A is always 1 inside the group.

  • Result

    F = B·C' + C·A

    Built from four NAND gates of one 74HC00.
